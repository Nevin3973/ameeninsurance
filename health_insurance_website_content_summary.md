# 1. Star Health {#1-star-health}

## A. Super Star Secure {#a-super-star-secure}

### Positioning

**Secure your health and that of your loved ones.**

Super Star is positioned as a comprehensive health-protection plan with
a broad set of built-in and optional benefits.

### Key Coverage & Benefits {#key-coverage--benefits}

-   **Sum Insured:** ₹7.5 lakh to ₹1 crore and above, with unlimited
    coverage options available depending on the plan/variant.
-   **Cumulative Bonus:** 50% of Sum Insured after each claim-free
    renewal, up to 100% of Sum Insured.
-   **Air Ambulance:** Covered up to ₹5 lakh in a policy year.
-   **Dental Check-up & Cleaning:** Consultation, IOPA X-ray and scaling
    for one person under each policy in the 2nd and 3rd policy years;
    available on a cashless basis.
-   **Organ Donor Expenses:** Covered up to the Sum Insured, including
    the donor\'s post-donation complications, subject to policy terms.
-   **Unlimited Tele-consultation:** Available through the STAR Health
    App.
-   **AI-Driven Face Scan:** Two times per month for each insured
    person.
-   **Modern Treatments:** Coverage for 10+ modern treatments, including
    bronchial thermoplasty, robotic surgery and vaporisation of the
    prostate, among others, up to the Sum Insured.
-   **Road Ambulance:** Covered up to the Sum Insured.
-   **Hospitalisation Expenses:** Anaesthesia, blood, oxygen, operation
    theatre and ICU charges covered up to the Sum Insured.
-   **Automatic Restoration of Sum Insured:** Up to 100% of Sum Insured,
    unlimited times in a policy year, for related and unrelated
    illnesses for subsequent hospitalisation.
-   **Day Care Procedures:** All day-care procedures covered up to the
    Sum Insured.
-   **Room, Boarding & Nursing Charges:**
    -   Sum Insured ₹7.5 lakh and above: No room-rent limit.
    -   Essential Plan ₹7.5 lakh and ₹10 lakh: Single private AC room.
    -   Value Plus: Up to ₹5,000 per day.
-   **AYUSH Treatment:** Covered up to the Sum Insured.
-   **Value-Added Services:** Discounts on pharmacy, diagnostics and
    consultations.
-   **Home Care Treatment:** Covered up to the Sum Insured.
-   **Freeze Your Age:** Premium is based on entry age until the first
    claim, subject to the applicable eligibility and age conditions.
-   **Domiciliary Hospitalisation:** Covered up to the Sum Insured.
-   **Pre- and Post-Hospitalisation:**
    -   Standard: 90 days before and up to 180 days after
        hospitalisation.
    -   Essential Plan: 30 days before and up to 60 days after
        hospitalisation.
-   **Premium Waiver:** One year\'s premium may be waived if the
    proposer, who is also insured, is diagnosed with a listed critical
    illness or dies due to an accident, subject to policy terms.
-   **STAR Wellness Program:** Earn wellness points through the STAR
    Health App / STAR Wellness App and redeem them for renewal-premium
    discounts, subject to programme conditions.
-   **E-Domestic Second Medical Opinion:** Access to a second medical
    opinion from a network doctor based on submitted medical records.

### Additional Coverage

-   **Grace Period Cover:** Coverage during the applicable renewal grace
    period, subject to policy terms.
-   **E-Connect:** Wellness services and international second medical
    opinion.
-   **Health Booster:** 100% additional Sum Insured after every block of
    7 claim-free years.
-   **Limitless Loyalty Bonus:** 100% additional Sum Insured at every
    renewal, subject to eligibility; applicable for age up to 60 years.
-   **Preventive Health Check-up:** Annual health check-up irrespective
    of claims, for eligible insured persons aged 18 years and above.

### Package A

-   **Nursing at Home:** Lump-sum benefit for each day of qualified
    nursing service.
    -   ₹1,000 cash limit per day.
-   **Durable Medical Equipment Cover:** Rental/purchase of specified
    medical equipment.
    -   Up to ₹1 lakh.
-   **Consumables Cover:** Coverage for listed non-medical consumables
    for admissible inpatient/day-care claims.

### Optional Covers

-   **Value Network:** Access to an extensive hospital network; 15%
    co-payment if treatment is taken outside the selected network.
-   **Voluntary Co-pay:** Premium discount by choosing a co-payment
    percentage.
-   **Limitless Care:** Unlimited Sum Insured for one eligible claim
    during the lifetime of the policy.
-   **Mamta -- Women Wellness Care:** Online health services; applicable
    to women aged 12 years and above.
-   **In-Clinic Consultation:** Specialist and GP consultation through
    the network; applicable to individuals aged 60 years and below.
-   **Maternity Expenses:** Delivery coverage including pre- and
    post-natal expenses, with a 2-year waiting period and newborn cover.
-   **Room Rent Modification:** Option to modify the room-rent/category
    option.
-   **Stay Fit:** Access to fitness centres in the network; applicable
    to ages 18--60.
-   **Voluntary Deductible:** Premium discount by opting for an
    aggregate deductible.

### Eligibility / Policy Type {#eligibility--policy-type}

-   Individual
-   Family
-   Husband and wife
-   Children

recommended under other variants such as Activ Fit or Activ One
VIP/VIP+. citeturn4search1turn4search2

The current New India prospectus confirms maternity and
infertility-related benefits with Sum Insured-dependent limits. For
example, maternity coverage ranges from up to ₹25,000 for the lower Sum

# Important Website Publishing Notes

1.  Do not present every benefit as unconditional. Use phrases such as
    **"subject to policy terms, limits and eligibility"** where
    appropriate.
2.  Keep **base benefits** and **optional covers** clearly separated.
3.  Display waiting periods wherever maternity, pre-existing diseases,
    infertility, specific diseases or other restricted benefits are
    mentioned.
4.  Avoid using "best", "unbeatable", "highest claim settlement ratio"
    or similar superlatives unless the statement is supported by
    current, verifiable evidence.
5.  The supplied Aditya Birla NRI creative shows a 15% / 20% / 25%
    discount ladder. Treat this as campaign material until the current
    product schedule confirms the exact figures.
6.  The supplied Aditya Birla Activ One MAX material mentions **10X
    Super Credit** and **1-year maternity waiting**. Current official
    Activ One MAX information found during research describes Super
    Credit differently and does not list maternity as a standard Activ
    One MAX benefit. This needs product-version verification before
    publication. citeturn4search0turn4search1
7.  The New India brochure supplied in the reference material is clearly
    branded **New India Assurance**, not United India. Therefore the
    United India section above is a separate web-researched section and
    should not be presented as part of the supplied Yuva Bharat
    creative.
8.  Insurance is a regulated product. The final website should link or
    refer users to the applicable brochure, prospectus, policy wording
    and Customer Information Sheet.
